## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an ultra-reliable, institutional, and reassuring environment engineered for critical clinical contexts. The brand persona blends cutting-edge algorithmic intelligence with the calm, unwavering authority of top-tier medical facilities. Every interaction must project zero-latency clarity, immaculate hygiene, and total data integrity.

Target audiences include emergency room triage teams, cross-institutional medical specialists, and patients in urgent care transitions. The visual language rejects decorative clutter in favor of high-legibility data hierarchy, tactile pill-shaped containment, and focused emergency signaling. Drawing from modern clinical minimalism and high-density institutional software, the interface leverages pure white operational surfaces, cool cyan-tinted atmospheric depth, and unmistakable status alerts that guide critical decisions under cognitive strain.

## Colors

The system uses `#0284C7` (Precision Cyan) as the primary focal tone, delivering high-contrast operational feedback, active focus indicators, and key touch targets. `#0A4572` (Deep Clinical Blue) anchors structural headers, identity anchors, and top-tier institutional elements. `#06B6D4` (Vital Cyan) provides telemetry accents and diagnostic status highlighting.

Neutral foundations rely on `#F8FAFC` for clinical canvas backdrops, `#FFFFFF` for elevated diagnostic cards, and slate tones (`#64748B` to `#0F172A`) for medical typography. Critical triage states are reserved strictly for semantic workflows:
- Emergency Critical: `#DC2626` text and borders over `#FEE2E2` fills.
- Triage Warning: `#D97706` text over `#FEF3C7` fills.
- Diagnostic Validated / Stable: `#059669` text over `#D1FAE5` fills.

Interactive states must maintain a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 against card backgrounds, scaling to 7:1 for critical vital statistics and patient identification badges.

## Typography

Typography establishes an uncompromising hierarchy between structural administrative metadata and patient vital data. `Manrope` governs all headings with balanced, geometric forms that project stability and clinical composure. `Hanken Grotesk` drives all reading copy, lab entries, dosage notes, and operational labels due to its open apertures and crisp rendering across dense mobile interfaces.

Data points such as heart rates, blood gas levels, and medical record numbers must employ tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent optical shift during real-time streaming updates. On viewports below 480px, `headline-xl` transitions to `headline-xl-mobile` to maintain balanced screen budgets without causing unintended line wraps in critical patient titles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built around a predictable, touch-optimized fluid vertical flow tailored for mobile handheld usage and tablet clinical stations. The core mobile canvas uses a 4-column structure with `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters, expanding to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 12-column fixed grid (`margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1rem`) on desktop management terminals.

Spacing enforces clear visual compartmentalization:
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro-spacing between paired labels and medical values.
- `space-sm` (8px): Gaps between status badges, filter chips, and interactive icon containers.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard internal card padding, input field padding, and list item row separation.
- `space-lg` (24px): Structural distancing between distinct diagnostic sections and patient timeline groups.
- `space-xl` (32px): Primary section demarcation and bottom fixed-action dock clearances.

All primary touch targets across mobile views enforce a strict minimum clearance of 48px by 48px to guarantee error-free triage operation under rapid clinical transit.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through surgical, cool-toned ambient shadows and pure white surface containment layered above `#F8FAFC` base canvases. Blur radii remain tightly controlled to avoid visual blurriness in clinical environments.

- **Level 0 (Flat/Substrate):** `#F8FAFC` background canvas; divider lines using 1px solid `#E2E8F0`.
- **Level 1 (Clinical Cards & Patient Rows):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces with subtle border `1px solid #E2E8F0` coupled with an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Overlays & Floating Navigation):** Floating bottom navigation docks, persistent emergency banners, and selected record drawers use `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Urgent Dialogs & Emergency Overlays):** Modal sheets and critical drug-interaction warnings take `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)` with a backdrop blur of `4px` across `#0F172A` at 40% opacity.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a pill-shaped, fully rounded design language (level 3) that softens complex clinical data, reduces visual fatigue, and establishes distinct visual boundaries for high-speed touch interactions.

- Standard buttons, badges, chips, search bars, and input fields utilize full pill geometry (`1rem` / fully rounded edge radius).
- Patient cards, triage modules, and test panel containers apply `2rem` (`rounded-lg`) corner radii to maintain clear content grouping while matching the pill ethos.
- Floating bottom sheets, persistent navigation docks, and full-screen diagnostic sheets utilize `3rem` (`rounded-xl`) on their top-leading edges to create friendly, ergonomic mobile interaction boundaries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#0284C7` with `#FFFFFF` text, pill curvature, 48px height, `1.5rem` horizontal padding. Active state darkens to `#0369A1`.
- **Emergency Action:** Filled `#DC2626` with `#FFFFFF` text, pill shape, high visual weight, accompanied by a vibrating or pulsing state during unacknowledged triage codes.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Outlined with `1.5px solid #0284C7`, `#0284C7` text, transparent background. Active state triggers 10% `#0284C7` tint fill.

### Chips & Status Badges
- Encapsulated pills with a fixed height of 28px for badges, 36px for selectable filter chips.
- Triage Emergency: `#FEE2E2` fill with `#DC2626` text, dot status indicator (8px circle).
- Triage Warning: `#FEF3C7` fill with `#D97706` text.
- Verified AI / Stable: `#D1FAE5` fill with `#059669` text.

### Cards (Patient Records & Lab Panels)
- `#FFFFFF` background, `2rem` (`rounded-lg`) corners, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, padding of `1.25rem`.
- Divided internally with subtle `#F1F5F9` lines. Header contains patient MRN and telemetry indicator, body hosts vital parameters, footer contains cross-hospital origin badge (e.g., "General Hospital Network via MedZex Node").

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Fully rounded pill geometry (`1rem`), 48px height, `#FFFFFF` surface with `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`.
- On focus: border shifts to `#0284C7` with a matching 3px soft aura (`rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)`).
- Trailing icons indicate AI syntax parsing or biometric verification states.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Pill-compatible radio targets: 22px concentric circles with `#0284C7` fill on check.
- Checkboxes: 20px rounded squares (`0.375rem` radius) with high-contrast `#0284C7` fill and white checkmark icon.

### Tab Navigation (Mobile Bottom Bar)
- Persistent floating dock elevated at `Level 2`, positioned with `1rem` margin from screen bottom, pill-encapsulated (`rounded-xl`).
- 64px total height, `#FFFFFF` surface, holding 4–5 core actions (Records, AI Triage, Emergency Access, Hospital Vault, Profile). Active tab receives a localized `#0284C7` pill indicator with white icon/label.

### Emergency Drawer
- Quick-release drawer accessible from any mobile screen via a top-right persistent red pulse trigger. Slides down with `2rem` bottom radius, providing zero-auth immediate access to blood type, severe allergies, and primary contact overrides.
